Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get a quote from Crown Packing And Shipping?

Simply fill out the contact form above or call us at 9953900400. We will get back to you within 30 minutes with a free, detailed quotation. For accurate pricing, we may arrange a free survey of your home or office.

Do you provide transit insurance for goods?

Yes, we provide comprehensive transit insurance for all goods transported through our services. This covers damage or loss during transit. Please discuss insurance options with our team when booking.

How many days in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 7-10 days in advance for local moves and 15-20 days for long-distance relocations. However, we can accommodate urgent moves with shorter notice whenever possible.

Do you pack items like electronics and fragile items?

Absolutely! Our professional packers are specially trained to handle electronics, glassware, artwork, antiques and all fragile items. We use high-quality bubble wrap, foam padding and specialized cartons to ensure maximum protection.

Do you offer storage/warehousing services?

Yes, we offer both short-term and long-term warehousing solutions at our secure facility in Greater Noida West. Our warehouse is equipped with CCTV surveillance and proper ventilation. Learn more about our warehousing services.
