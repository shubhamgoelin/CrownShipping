Our Logistics Service Portfolio

Road Freight Transportation

Our core logistics service is road freight transportation for businesses. We provide Full Truckload (FTL) and Less Than Truckload (LTL) services on all major routes across India. Our fleet includes various vehicle sizes from small tempos and mini-trucks for small shipments to large 22-foot and 32-foot trailers for bulk freight. All vehicles are GPS-tracked and operated by experienced, licensed commercial drivers.

Express and Time-Critical Delivery

For businesses that need urgent or time-critical freight delivery, we offer express logistics solutions with guaranteed delivery windows. Our express service operates on all major routes out of Greater Noida and Noida, ensuring your time-sensitive shipments reach their destination on schedule. We operate 24/7 for urgent requests.

B2B and B2C Logistics Solutions

Whether you're a manufacturer distributing to retailers (B2B) or an e-commerce brand delivering to end customers (B2C), we have tailored logistics solutions for your business model. Our B2B logistics covers distributor and warehouse replenishment, while our B2C service includes e-commerce fulfillment, returns management and customer delivery coordination.

Supply Chain Management

Beyond transportation, we offer complete supply chain management services including vendor freight coordination, inbound logistics management, inventory optimization support and distribution network planning. Our supply chain team works as an extension of your operations team to identify inefficiencies and implement improvements.

Last Mile Delivery

Last mile delivery is often the most expensive and complex part of the supply chain. Our dedicated last mile delivery team in Greater Noida and surrounding areas provides reliable, customer-friendly delivery services with real-time tracking and delivery notifications. We specialize in furniture, appliance and large item last mile delivery for e-commerce businesses.

Industrial and Heavy Cargo Logistics

For manufacturing companies in Greater Noida's industrial areas, we offer specialized heavy cargo logistics including plant and machinery movement, raw material supply logistics and finished goods distribution. We have experience handling oversized loads, heavy equipment and industrial components that require specialized vehicles and equipment.
