Types of Two-Wheelers We Transport

Motorcycles and Sport Bikes

From standard commuter motorcycles to high-performance sport bikes, our team is equipped to handle them all. Sport bikes with low ground clearance are handled with extra care using specialized loading ramps. Premium motorcycles like Royal Enfield, Harley Davidson, KTM, Ducati and Triumph are packed with additional protective padding to prevent any cosmetic damage.

Scooters and Mopeds

Scooters and mopeds are popular for city commuting and require the same level of protection during transport. Their light weight and compact size make them easier to handle, but they are just as prone to cosmetic damage if not properly packed. Our team wraps all scooters with protective foam and secures them firmly during transit.

Electric Two-Wheelers

The growing popularity of electric scooters and bikes means we receive an increasing number of requests for EV two-wheeler transport. Electric vehicles require special handling as their batteries must be properly prepared for transport. Our team is trained in EV two-wheeler transport protocols to ensure safe and compliant transportation.

How to Prepare Your Bike for Transport

  • Clean the bike thoroughly and photograph its current condition from all angles
  • Drain fuel to less than 1/4 tank to minimize fire hazard
  • Check and top up coolant and engine oil levels
  • Remove all personal accessories and loose items
  • Deflate tires slightly (about 10%) to accommodate pressure changes during transit
  • Disconnect the battery if instructed by our team
  • Remove the side panniers and luggage if any
  • Note the odometer reading before handover
