Types of Car Transport Carriers We Use

Enclosed Car Carriers

For luxury vehicles, vintage cars, premium SUVs and any vehicle requiring the highest level of protection, we offer fully enclosed car transport. Enclosed carriers protect your vehicle from weather conditions, road debris and dust during transit. This option is ideal for vehicles with special paint jobs, custom modifications or high monetary/sentimental value.

Open Car Carriers / Hydraulic Carriers

For standard vehicles, our open car carrier service provides safe and economical transportation. These hydraulic carriers use specialized ramps that allow vehicles to be loaded and unloaded without driving, eliminating any risk of undercarriage damage. The car is securely strapped to prevent movement during transit.

Flatbed Truck Transport

For oversized vehicles, modified vehicles or those that cannot be driven, our flatbed truck service provides a safe transport option. The vehicle is carefully loaded using a winch and secured with heavy-duty straps throughout the journey.

Tips to Prepare Your Car for Transport

To ensure a smooth car transport experience, here are some steps you should take before handing over your vehicle:

  • Clean your vehicle thoroughly and take photographs of its current condition
  • Remove all personal belongings and valuables from the car
  • Ensure the fuel tank is less than 1/4 full
  • Check tire pressure and inflate to recommended levels
  • Disable the car alarm to avoid it triggering during transit
  • Remove external accessories like antennas, roof racks and bike carriers
  • Note down the odometer reading and share it with our team
  • Provide spare keys to our transport team
