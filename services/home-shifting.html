Specialized Packing for Different Items in Your Home

Furniture Packing and Moving

Large furniture pieces like sofas, beds, wardrobes and dining sets require special handling. Our team first assesses each piece to determine the best disassembly approach. Wooden furniture is wrapped in thick moving blankets to prevent scratches. Metal parts are individually wrapped to prevent dents. We use professional tools to disassemble and reassemble all types of modular furniture, beds and storage systems.

Electronics and Appliances

Television sets, home theatre systems, refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioners and other electronic appliances require careful handling and proper packing. We use anti-static wrapping for all electronics. Large appliances are secured with foam corners and wrapped in stretch film. Air conditioners are handled by trained technicians who disconnect and reconnect units at both locations.

Kitchen Items and Crockery

The kitchen is often the most challenging room to pack due to the variety of fragile, heavy and oddly shaped items. Our kitchen packing specialists individually wrap each piece of crockery in packing paper. Heavy kitchen appliances are packed in double-walled boxes. Knives and sharp objects are safely packed in separate containers with proper labeling.

Fragile and Antique Items

For artwork, antiques, crystal collections, decorative items and other fragile pieces, we use custom packaging with extra cushioning. These items receive individual attention and are clearly marked as fragile for extra care during loading, transport and unloading.

Clothing and Personal Belongings

We use wardrobe boxes with hanging rails to transport clothes without creasing. Personal items are packed in clearly labeled boxes by category. Important documents are packed separately with waterproof protection.

Tips for a Smooth Home Shifting Experience

  • Book your movers at least 7-10 days in advance to get the best rates and availability
  • Declutter before the move - donate or dispose of items you no longer need
  • Pack an essentials box with items you'll need on the first night in your new home
  • Keep important documents, jewelry and valuables with you personally
  • Inform utility companies and update your address with relevant authorities
  • Take photographs of your electronic setup before disconnecting
  • Be present on moving day to supervise and answer questions
