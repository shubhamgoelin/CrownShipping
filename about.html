Who We Are - Crown Packing And Shipping

Crown Packing And Shipping is a professionally managed, fully licensed relocation and logistics company headquartered in Greater Noida West, Uttar Pradesh. Established with a vision to provide world-class packing and moving services at affordable prices, we have grown to become one of the most trusted names in the packers and movers industry across the Delhi NCR region.

With our head office at Plot No-148, VPO- Khairpur Gurjar, Sector-3 Greater Noida West, we are strategically located to serve customers across Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Delhi, Faridabad, Gurugram, and all major cities of India. Our team of trained professionals ensures that every relocation project, whether big or small, is handled with the highest level of care and precision.

At Crown Packing And Shipping, we believe that moving your home or office should be a smooth, stress-free experience. That is why we offer end-to-end relocation services - from professional packing using premium quality materials, to safe transportation using our modern fleet of vehicles, to unpacking and rearrangement at the destination. We also provide specialized services including car transport, bike transport, warehousing, and supply chain logistics.

Our Strengths

What Makes Us the Best Packers and Movers in Greater Noida

Over the years, we have built our reputation on three pillars: Safety, Reliability and Affordability. Here is why thousands of customers across NCR trust us.

1. Certified and Licensed Operations

Crown Packing And Shipping is a legally registered company with all necessary licenses and permits for operating a professional packing and moving business in India. We comply with all regulatory requirements including IBA approved transit insurance, which means your goods are fully protected during transit. Our registration and compliance documents are always available for customer review.

2. Professional and Trained Workforce

Our team of packers, loaders, drivers and supervisors undergoes rigorous training before being deployed on any project. We invest heavily in employee development because we understand that the quality of our service depends directly on the expertise and attitude of our team. From properly packing a fragile crystal vase to dismantling and reassembling large furniture, our team handles every task with professionalism.

3. Modern Fleet of Vehicles

We operate a large fleet of GPS-equipped trucks, container vehicles, tempos and mini trucks of various capacities. All our vehicles are regularly serviced and maintained to the highest standards. Whether you're moving a small studio apartment or a 5-bedroom villa, we have the right vehicle for your needs. Our vehicles are equipped with padding and straps to ensure goods remain stable during transit.

4. Comprehensive Transit Insurance

Despite our best efforts, unforeseen accidents can occasionally occur during transit. That is why we provide comprehensive transit insurance for all goods transported through our services. This gives our customers complete peace of mind knowing that in the unlikely event of any damage, they are fully covered. We handle insurance claims promptly and transparently.

5. Pan India Network

With a network spanning over 500 cities across India, Crown Packing And Shipping can facilitate seamless relocations from and to any part of the country. Our network of trusted partners and associates ensures that even the most remote destinations are accessible. Whether you're moving within Greater Noida or relocating to Chennai, Mumbai or Kolkata, we've got you covered.

6. Customer-Centric Approach

At Crown Packing And Shipping, the customer always comes first. From the moment you make your first inquiry to the final delivery of your goods, our team is with you every step of the way. We provide dedicated customer support, regular shipment updates, and are always available to address any concerns. Our goal is not just to complete a move, but to create a customer for life.
